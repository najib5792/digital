# Medical Benefits ATM — paper-cutout stop-motion (20 s, 9:16)

`output/medical-atm-paper-cutout.mp4` — 1080×1920, 24 fps (12 stop-motion poses/sec, each held 2 frames), AAC SFX track.

Everything is generated by `render.py` — no image is panned or zoomed. Every element (ATM body, sign,
screen pieces, keys, card, legs, arrow, gears, particles, check, confetti, cardboard RM1,000,000 plaque,
receipt, hand, thumb, rays) is built as its own layered paper cutout (grain texture, lighter cut edge,
darker card-thickness rim, contact shadow) and moves independently with per-pose "boil" jitter.

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | ATM shuffles in (move → stop → move → stop → settle), screen wakes (face → INSERT MEDICAL CARD), red arrow bounces at the slot |
| 0:03–0:07 | Medical card walks in from the left on paper legs (step, step, wobble, step, stop), legs poof away, card tilts and slides into the slot, ATM bounces |
| 0:07–0:11 | PROCESSING…: chunky gears, progress bar 0→25→50→75→100 %, ATM shakes, paper particles vibrate, then a dead stop for ~0.3 s |
| 0:11–0:14 | Green check POPs, APPROVED!, confetti burst, ATM hops; YOUR MEDICAL LIMIT + stacked-cardboard RM1,000,000 pops forward |
| 0:14–0:18 | Receipt slot rattles, receipt prints out pose by pose (MEDICAL LIMIT / RM1,000,000), paper hand grabs and pulls it out |
| 0:18–0:20 | Hero hold: receipt centred with paper rays, ATM behind, stepped push-in, final hold |

SFX (synthesised): paper slides, footsteps, insertion click, processing ticks, ding, confetti pop/crackle, receipt printer, final soft impact.
