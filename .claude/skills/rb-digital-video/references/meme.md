# 50. Meme format (recipe — not yet tested)

**Look:** Classic meme layouts built from our own images: top/bottom caption, 'Expectation vs Reality', two-panel reaction, drake-style approve/reject (with our own illustrated character, not the real meme photo), zoom-punch on the punchline, reaction face pop.

**Best for:** Content lucu relatable, humor bisnes/takaful, engagement ringan.

## Flow prompts
Reaction images of an ORIGINAL character (no real people, no copyrighted meme photos): `A 2x2 grid of 4 separate cartoon reaction images of the same character <description>, expressions: smug approval, disgusted rejection, shocked, crying laugh, plain background, no text, thin white gutters, square 1:1.`
Follow common.md sheet rules (no text in images, shared style line, sheet 1 as ingredient for sheet 2).

## Build (common.py)
Layouts drawn: white panels, black impact-style captions (anton with thick black stroke white fill), panel split lines. Beats: setup panel appears → pause → punchline panel slams + zoom-punch 1.15→1 + shake. Chain 3–5 memes per video with quick whoosh transitions.

## Audio (Mix)
Comedy music 0.25, record-scratch-ish (fast pitch-down sweep) before punchlines, boing/pop; VO can be minimal.
