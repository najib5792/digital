# 29. Blueprint / pelan teknikal (recipe — not yet tested)

**Look:** Blue grid paper, white line drawings that trace themselves, dimension lines with arrows and measurements, callout labels, parts exploding apart then assembling.

**Best for:** Terangkan produk/sistem (AutoSocial architecture, rumah, gadget), 'bagaimana ia berfungsi'.

## Flow prompts
4x4 sheet: `…separate technical blueprint line drawings, white lines on dark blue background, engineering style, no text, no labels…`.
Follow common.md sheet rules (no text in images, shared style line, sheet 1 as ingredient for sheet 2).

## Build (common.py)
Background drawn in code: #0b3d91-ish blue + fine and major grid lines. Cut drawings (bright pixels on blue → alpha). Line reveal via sweep rank. Dimension lines: drawn arrows + text (oswald/typewriter). Explode: parts translate outward then back with ease.

## Audio (Mix)
Pencil scribble, tech blips, lofi/ambient 0.2.
