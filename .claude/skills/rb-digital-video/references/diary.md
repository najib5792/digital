# 23. Diari / jurnal tulisan tangan (recipe — not yet tested)

**Look:** Lined journal pages, handwritten entries write themselves in ink (caveat), taped photos/stickers, doodles in margins, coffee stains; page flips between days ('Hari 1', 'Hari 7').

**Best for:** Kisah peribadi, journey kurus/simpan duit/bisnes, '30 hari cuba…', vlog-style tanpa muka.

## Flow prompts
Background: `Vertical 9:16 open journal notebook page with faint blue lines and red margin, top-down, empty, no text`. Photos/stickers: 2x2 sheet or 4x4 sticker sheet.
Follow common.md sheet rules (no text in images, shared style line, sheet 1 as ingredient for sheet 2).

## Build (common.py)
Text reveal by x-rank mask per line with pen tip dot at the front; date header in marker; photos with masking tape strips (drawn) and slight rotation; flip = page slide up with curl shadow.

## Audio (Mix)
Pen scribble while writing, paper flip, lofi music 0.25.
