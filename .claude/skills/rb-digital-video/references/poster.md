# 35. Poster vintage / propaganda (recipe — not yet tested)

**Look:** Bold vintage poster art (limited palette, halftone), huge slogans in condensed type, sunburst rays rotating behind, paper texture, stamp 'LULUS'/'PENTING' slams.

**Best for:** Seruan tindakan, awareness (contoh 'Lindungi keluarga!'), promo retro, motivasi.

## Flow prompts
2x2 sheet: `…separate vintage propaganda poster illustrations, bold limited palette red cream black, halftone shading, heroic composition, <subjects>, no text…`.
Follow common.md sheet rules (no text in images, shared style line, sheet 1 as ingredient for sheet 2).

## Build (common.py)
Each poster fills screen (crop square centre to 9:16 with extension using rays behind). Rays: rotating sunburst polygons behind cut-out figure (or just behind image with vignette). Slogans anton/bebas with letter-by-letter slam, stamp = red rotated rect text with grunge mask (noise).

## Audio (Mix)
Brass-ish stabs (layered saw chords), slam on words, stamp thud; tense or heroic drone.
