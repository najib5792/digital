# 34. Flat motion graphic (corporate) (recipe — not yet tested)

**Look:** Minimal flat shapes (circles, blobs, lines) morph and slide, icons and short text blocks animate in with smooth easing, brand colours, clean transitions (shape wipes).

**Best for:** Explainer syarikat, promo SaaS, pengenalan servis, onboarding.

## Flow prompts
Icon sheet 4x4 (flat vector). Everything else drawn in code.
Follow common.md sheet rules (no text in images, shared style line, sheet 1 as ingredient for sheet 2).

## Build (common.py)
Palette of 3 brand colours; transitions = big circle expanding from a point to fill screen (new bg colour), or diagonal band wipe. Text: poppins bold/regular, lines slide up with mask reveal (crop). Icons pop with ease_back. Keep lots of whitespace.

## Audio (Mix)
Clean corporate 'lofi'/'cheerful' 0.25, soft whoosh + pop.
