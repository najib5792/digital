# 15. Trailer filem (recipe — not yet tested)

**Look:** Letterbox bars (2.39:1 look inside 9:16 → bars top/bottom), dark cinematic stills with slow push-in, black title cards with big spaced serif/condensed text fading in ('TAHUN 2026…', 'SATU KEPUTUSAN…'), hard cuts on booms, final logo + 'AKAN DATANG' / date.

**Best for:** Launch produk, promo event/kelas, cerita dramatik, teaser.

## Flow prompts
2 sheets 2x2: `…separate cinematic film stills, dramatic lighting, anamorphic look, teal and orange grade, <scenes>, no text, thin white gutters, square 1:1.`
Follow common.md sheet rules (no text in images, shared style line, sheet 1 as ingredient for sheet 2).

## Build (common.py)
Alternate: still (2–3 s, Ken Burns + grain + vignette) → black title card (1.2–2 s, text letter-spacing animates wider, fade in/out). Build-up: cuts get faster (1 s → 0.4 s) towards climax, then silence 0.8 s, then final title slam. Letterbox height ≈ 25% top & bottom.

## Audio (Mix)
Boom on every title card, riser before climax, braam (low detuned saw 45 Hz, 2 s), silence gap, final hit. Music: drone + tense.
